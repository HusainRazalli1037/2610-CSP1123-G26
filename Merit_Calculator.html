SPM MERIT CALCULATION GUIDE
CALCULATION FORMULA
  • Compulsory Subject (40%): BM + BI + MATEMATIK + SEJARAH
  • Best Package Subject (30%): 2 subject based on stream
  • Best Subject (10%): 2 additional subject (if any)
  • Co-curricular (10%): Co-curricular activity marks
  • Total MERIT = ((Total/90) * 90) + Co-curriculum
CALCULATION STEPS
  1. Choose stream type (STEM A/B/C or KSI)
  2. Select grades for 4 compulsory subjects
  3. Select 2 elective subjects based on stream
  4. Select additional subjects (if applicable)
  5. Select co-curricular marks (0.00 - 10.00)
  6. MERIT score will be calculated automatically
